## Observations

- Agencies that appear for the head terms have a dedicated "full-service Amazon agency" page or homepage title that says it plainly. Albert Scott's homepage title is "Home - Albert Scott".
- Several agencies rank a "best Amazon agencies" article on their own domain. This is a content type Albert Scott does not have.
- Directories (Clutch, Nova, Sermondo) take part of the "best" query. Presence on those is off-site work and belongs in Stage 3.
