# Report, 2026-09-03: first full run of all three stages

For: Albert Scott leadership and whoever edits albertscott.com. Detail lives in `../audits/stage1-audit-2026-09-03.md`, `../roadmap/roadmap-2026-09-03.md`, `../growth/growth-plan-2026-09-03.md`.

## Where the site stands

- 86 content pages were rendered and graded on 12 points. Average score is 5.48 of 11. The best pages are the five blog articles (7.5 to 8.0). The homepage scores 7.5, the four division pages 5.0 to 7.5, and 32 test, legacy and duplicate pages score 2.5 to 5.0.
- Albert Scott appeared in 3 of 24 searches, all of them queries containing its own name. For "full service Amazon agency", "Amazon agency", "Amazon advertising agency", "Amazon DSP agency", "Amazon Vendor Central management agency" and "Amazon agency New York" it did not appear in the returned results. For its own name the homepage was result 8, behind ZoomInfo, LinkedIn and 2024 press releases.
- In five answer-engine style prompts, Albert Scott was named once (agencies handling both Vendor Central and Seller Central), and the source was a third-party article, not the site.

## The five things that explain most of it

1. Titles do not say what the company does. 68 of 86 titles are a page label plus "Albert Scott" ("Home", "Listing Division", "DSP"). Every competitor page that ranks puts the service in the title.
2. A third of the indexable site should not exist. 32 pages are tests, 2019-era leftovers showing raw shortcode, or duplicates of current pages (an old homepage, two Services pages, two Thank You pages, a second Marketing Division). 33 pages have no internal links pointing to them at all.
3. The service pages are thin and disconnected. Division pages run 336 to 706 words. The DSP page, the best-written service page, has one inbound link. The homepage reaches its own division pages through redirects.
4. Images carry no information. 1,504 of 1,601 images have empty alt text. The 23 portfolio pages are image galleries with 10 to 14 words each.
5. The company entity is inconsistent. Two schema blocks disagree on size and description, no page links to LinkedIn or the Forbes coverage, and the marketing slug is misspelled "devision" in the URL, title and breadcrumb.

## What was decided

- Stage 2 roadmap: 20 items in three batches. Batch 1 (week 1) is configuration and short copy: retitle 8 primary pages, write missing meta descriptions, noindex 24 archive URLs, fix the redirecting links, rename the misspelled slug, merge the schema, set og:image. Batch 2 consolidates the 32 triage pages and fixes headings, alt text and internal links on primary pages. Batch 3 deepens the division pages and case studies.
- Stage 3 growth plan: fix the entity first, rebuild rather than add service pages, publish two comparison articles the competitors already have, expand the location page for New York, get listed on Nova, Sermondo and Clutch, convert nine case study PDFs into HTML pages.
- Nothing has been changed on the live site. All items are proposals for the site owner.

## What to watch

Weekly: 6 core queries, 2 GEO prompts, render check, sitemap and counts. Monthly: full re-grade of touched pages and all 24 queries. Targets are in the growth plan's Measurement section.
